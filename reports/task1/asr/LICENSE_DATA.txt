References and predictions for VIVOS test

Audio and reference transcripts originate from VIVOS, prepared by AILAB at VNUHCM University of Science.
Official source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/AILAB-VNUHCM/vivos
Dataset license: Creative Commons Attribution NonCommercial ShareAlike 4.0.

No raw audio files or model weights are included in the handover archive.
